The Story

Not a pitch.
A proof point.

June 5, 2026

On June 5, 2026, the HKPC All Blacks took the floor against the RHPC Hookers in the inaugural match of the NYC Interborough League. We won. The bar buzzed. The Botanist signed on as the first official sponsor — and the inaugural Botanist All Black cocktail launched the same night.

The All Blacks are the engine behind the Metropolitan League — club vs. club, borough vs. borough. Year 1 is New York. The founding clubs are seated.
